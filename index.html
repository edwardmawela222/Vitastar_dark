Our Practice

Vitastar Medical Centre

Your trusted family doctor for everyday healthcare, clear advice, and peace of mind. Led by Dr. A.H. Narotam, Vitastar Medical Centre provides accessible primary healthcare for individuals and families in the Boksburg community.

Our practice focuses on practical medical care, straightforward advice, and continuity of care for patients of all ages — whether you're dealing with an illness, managing a chronic condition, or simply looking for guidance about your health.

We believe healthcare should be approachable, respectful, and centred around the needs of each patient.

Meet the Doctor

Dr. A.H. Narotam

Dr. Narotam has been in healthcare since 2007, with a lasting focus on primary healthcare and community medicine. He completed his medical degree at the University of the Witwatersrand. Years of close patient work have shaped a simple philosophy: patients should always be treated as people, not numbers.

He believes compassion and understanding are central to good healthcare — no one asks to become unwell, and seeking care should feel supportive, not stressful. As part of widening access to healthcare, the practice also offers virtual consultations for advice and follow-up care when an in-person visit isn't necessary.
